    Nakuru on your doorstep

    Distances are from the resort. The travel desk can arrange transport and game drives.

    0.8 km

    Lake Nakuru National Park

    Flamingos and pelicans on the lake shore, white and black rhino, Rothschild’s giraffe and lions in the acacia woodland. Baboon Cliff gives the classic view over the lake.

    4.7 km

    Hyrax Hill

    A prehistoric site with remains of settlements going back thousands of years, a small museum and views across the lake basin.

    12.5 km

    Menengai Crater

    One of the largest volcanic calderas in Africa. Drive or hike to the rim for a wide view of the crater floor.

    32 km

    Lake Elmenteita

    A shallow soda lake in the Rift Valley, part of the Kenya Lake System World Heritage Site, popular for birdwatching.

    34.5 km

    Kariandusi

    A prehistoric site known for stone hand axes, with a site museum near Lake Elmenteita.

    Questions guests ask

    What time are check in and check out?

    Check in from 1:00 pm. Check out by 10:30 am.

    Is breakfast included?

    Yes, a buffet breakfast comes with bed and breakfast, half board and full board plans.

    How far is Lake Nakuru National Park?

    Under a kilometre from the resort.

    Do you have rates for Kenyan residents?

    Yes. Resident and non-resident rates differ, so share where you’re visiting from when you book.

    Can I bring my own tent?

    Yes. The campsite is secure and private, and tents and bedding are available if you need them.

    Are pets allowed?

    Pets aren’t allowed at the resort.
