This policy explains what personal data Kivu Resort collects through this website, why, who we share it with and the rights you have under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who we are

2. What we collect

  • Room bookings: your name, phone number, optional email, residency (resident or non-resident, for pricing), dates, number of guests, meal plan and any requests you type.
  • Payments: the amount and the M-Pesa confirmation code you give us. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN or card details.
  • Food orders, pool, gym, Kids Park, package, safari and event requests: what you ordered or asked for, the date, number of people, where to bring your order and your contact details.
  • Food preferences or allergies: only if you choose to tell us, and only with your explicit consent (see section 4).
  • Guest login: your booking reference and the 6-digit PIN we give you.
  • Reviews: the first name, rating and review you submit.
  • Messages: what you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Technical data: small items saved in your browser (see our Cookie Policy) and standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security. Logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Please don't send ID, passport or card numbers through this website. Kenyan law requires hotels to register guests, so we ask for identification at the front desk when you check in, not online.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To take and manage your bookings and orders, confirm them, prepare your room and food, and contact you about them: to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep financial and tax records and meet guest-registration duties: to comply with the law.
  • To keep the website and our guests safe, prevent fraud and answer your enquiries: our legitimate interests.
  • To send you offers and news, publish your review, use allergy details and remember your details on your device: only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Sensitive data

Food allergies and some dietary needs are health information, which the Act treats as sensitive. We only collect it if you type it in and tick the box giving us explicit consent. We use it only to prepare your meals safely, share it only with our kitchen team, and delete it when your stay or order ends.

5. Children

This website is for adults. Kids Park, family and school bookings must be made by a parent, guardian or the school, and we only ask how many children are coming. Please don't type children’s names, ages or other details into notes. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We share it only when needed, with:

  • Kivu staff who need it to serve you (front desk, kitchen, tours team).
  • Our website hosting provider [provider name] and booking system provider [provider name], who store data for us under contract.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa), when you choose to pay by M-Pesa.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms), only when you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Our email provider [provider name], when we email you.
  • Drivers and park or activity partners, who receive your name, phone number and pick-up point to run your trip.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Google Maps is not loaded on this website. If you click a map link, Google Maps opens in a new tab and Google’s own privacy policy applies.

If an AI chat assistant is switched on for this website, the questions you type are sent to [AI provider] to write a reply. Please don't type personal details into the chat.

7. Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our providers, such as WhatsApp and [hosting or email provider], may store data outside Kenya. We only use providers that give appropriate safeguards for your data, as the Act requires.

8. How long we keep it

  • Booking, payment and billing records: 5 years after your stay, as the Tax Procedures Act requires.
  • Enquiries that don’t become bookings: 12 months.
  • Allergy and dietary details: deleted when your stay or order ends.
  • Guest login details: deleted 90 days after check-out.
  • Reviews: until you ask us to remove them.
  • Marketing preferences: until you unsubscribe.

9. How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), guest stay details are stored encrypted with your reference and PIN, and only staff who need data can see it. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify the Data Commissioner within 72 hours and tell you where the risk to you is high.

10. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used,
  • get a copy of your data,
  • object to how we use it,
  • have wrong data corrected,
  • have data deleted when we no longer need it,
  • receive your data in a form you can move to someone else, and
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what we did before.

To use these rights, email info@kivuresort.com with the subject “Data request”. We may ask you to confirm who you are, and we will reply within the time the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021 set.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we review it at least once a year.

Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Draft for review. Every time limit and percentage marked like this must match what Kivu actually does before the site goes live.

Rooms (Kivu Resort and Msafiri Lodge)

  • Cancel 7 days or more before arrival: full refund of your deposit.
  • Cancel 3 to 6 days before arrival: 50% of your deposit is refunded.
  • Cancel less than 72 hours before arrival, or don’t arrive: your deposit is not refunded.
  • Leaving early: nights already booked are charged.
  • Easter, Christmas and New Year bookings may have different terms, which we tell you before you pay.

Pool, gym and Kids Park passes

  • Cancel before the day of your visit: full refund.
  • Passes are not refundable once the day has started, unless we close the facility, in which case we refund you or move your visit.

Food orders

  • Cancel before we start preparing your order: full refund.
  • If something is wrong with your order, tell us straight away and we will replace it or refund you.

Safaris, packages and events

  • Cancel 14 days or more before: full refund.
  • Cancel 7 to 13 days before: 50% refund.
  • Less than 7 days: no refund, except park fees we have not yet paid.
  • Events and team building follow the terms in your signed quotation.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel anything you booked, you choose between a full refund and moving to another date.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the M-Pesa number or method you paid with, within 7 working days of us agreeing the refund. To cancel or ask for a refund, contact us with your booking reference at info@kivuresort.com or +254 726 026 894.
